# Customer
+ The customer can Either make an account or shop as a guest.
+ When loading in to the front page as a Customer you will be able to see a bunch of cards and on those cards are the MeatShops selling ham thats signed with us and the nearest one shows first.
+ When the Customer clicks on the meatshop she wants to order from she gets a selection menue of selected hams "cured, raw, boiled and more" and when they click on one of them they can select a size.
After they have selected their ham and "Amount" they can choose to add additional items such as ribs, turkey maybe or other.
+ After making their order they will come to a page with their order assembled and they have to write down Name, Email and phonenumber if they arent already signed in, if they are signed in to an account it will be preapplied
and they can click the massive make order button.
+ The site will have both a dark and light mode.
+ The site will use both english and swedish language that you can switch between.
+ The customer can have a drop down menu in the corner with my information, my orders and logout.
+ when made an order it will show a green checkmark in a circle taking up the screen with "Your order has been booked"
